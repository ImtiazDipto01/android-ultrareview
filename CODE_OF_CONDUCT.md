# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Android UltraReview to be welcoming, technically rigorous, and respectful. Contributors should:

- critique ideas and observable behavior rather than people;
- explain claims with reproducible evidence;
- accept good-faith correction;
- respect different experience levels, identities, and communication styles; and
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and publication of private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile or an available private repository contact channel. Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the community.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and preserve reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible.
